Grammar patterns

PatternFormExample
AffirmativeBase verb + complementEnter your email address.
NegativeDon't + base verbDon't share your password.
Polite imperativePlease + base verbPlease select the correct option.
SequenceFirst / Next / Then / FinallyFirst, sign in. Then, check your inbox.
Inclusive suggestionLet's + base verbLet's review the details together.

Tone matters

Direct: Give me your account number.
Polite request: Could you please provide your account number?
Polite imperative: Please check the account number on your screen.

“Could you…?” is a request, not an imperative. Use it to soften customer-facing communication.

Step 4 of 5 · Recommended Time: 13 Mins

Internalize · Explain the Process

13 minutes · Practice in pairs. One student gives instructions; the other follows them.

Scenario A · Everyday life

Explain how to prepare a simple snack or find a location using a map. Give at least four ordered steps, one negative instruction, and one check-for-understanding question.

Scenario B · Digital support

Help your partner find an order confirmation email. Give step-by-step instructions using open, select, search, check, scroll. Do not ask for real passwords or private information.

Speaking support

First, … · Next, … · After that, … · Finally, … · Please … · Don’t … · Make sure to … · Were you able to find it? · Could you tell me what you see?

Timing: 2 min preparation · 8 min partner practice and role switch · 3 min feedback.

Listener: follow each instruction, ask for clarification if needed, and give one suggestion to make the process clearer.

Step 5 of 5 · Recommended Time: 15 Mins

Fluent Use · Guide the Customer

15 minutes · Work in pairs. The agent gives spoken instructions; the customer clicks through a simulated system and checks the final result.

How to role-play
  1. Student A is the agent. Open the Agent Brief and explain each step aloud without clicking for your partner.
  2. Student B is the customer. Follow the spoken instructions in the system below. Ask questions when something is unclear.
  3. The customer clicks Check My Actions to verify the result, then reads the feedback.
  4. Switch roles and complete the second scenario, which uses a different interface.

Use only the fictional numbers shown in the Agent Brief. The customer should not read the brief; the agent dictates the code or reference number aloud.

5-minute role-play timer

Start once for Scenario A, then reset for Scenario B when students switch roles.
