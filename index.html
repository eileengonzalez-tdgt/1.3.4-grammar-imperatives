STEP 2 · 12 MINUTES

Clarify · The Language of Instructions

Grammar Patterns

Affirmative: Base verb + complement
Enter your email address.

Negative: Don't + base verb
Don't share your password.

Polite imperative: Please + base verb
Please select the correct option.

Sequence: First, Next, Then, Finally
First, sign in. Then, check your inbox.

Inclusive suggestion: Let's + base verb
Let's review the details together.

Tone Matters

Direct: Give me your account number.

Polite imperative: Please check the account number on your screen.

Polite request: Could you please provide your account number?

Remember: “Could you…?” is a polite question, not an imperative.

STEP 4 · 13 MINUTES

Internalize · Explain the Process

Work in pairs. One student gives instructions and the other follows them.

Scenario A · Everyday Life

Explain how to prepare a simple snack or find a location using a map.

  • Give at least four ordered steps.
  • Include one negative imperative.
  • Check understanding.

Scenario B · Digital Support

Help your partner find an order confirmation email.

  • Use open, select, search, check and scroll.
  • Explain steps clearly.
  • Ask if your partner needs clarification.
