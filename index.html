02 · Clarify: Understanding Time Expressions

12 minutes · Notice how speakers describe when things happen.

Read: Planning a team celebration

Read with a partner. Take turns playing Maya and Jordan. Underline phrases that tell you when something happens.

Maya: Are you coming to the team celebration on Friday?
Jordan: Definitely! What are we celebrating?
Maya: It’s a double celebration: our team anniversary and your promotion.
Jordan: That’s exciting! What time does the party start?
Maya: It starts at 5:00, but we’ll set everything up around 4:00.
Jordan: Great. What should I do before the party?
Maya: Could you help arrange the chairs? We’ll prepare the snacks at the beginning of the afternoon.
Jordan: Sure! What will happen during the party?
Maya: First, the manager will give a short speech. Then we’ll have food and a surprise game.
Jordan: Nice. Is there anything special planned on the day of the event?
Maya: Yes! We’re bringing a cake. At the party, everyone can share a favorite team memory.
Jordan: And what happens after the party?
Maya: We’ll clean up together, and at the end we’ll take a group photo.
Jordan: Sounds perfect. I’ll be there before 4:00 to help!

Accuracy tip

Use at for clock times, on for days, during for an event or time period, and around for an approximate time.

Double celebration describes an event, not a time expression.

04 · Internalize: Tell Me What Happened

13 minutes · Work with a partner and build a clear timeline of a real or imagined event.

Round 1 · Interview

Partner A: Tell your partner about a celebration, appointment, or work event. Explain what happened before, during, and after it.

Partner B: Ask when it started, what happened next, and why it mattered.

Round 2 · Switch and compare

Switch roles. Listen for at least four time expressions. Together, retell one event using first, then, finally.

Optional: Adapt the dialogue you created in Vocabulary 1.2.1 by adding time expressions.

Useful follow-up questions

When did it happen? · What happened before that? · What happened during the event? · What happened afterward? · About what time did it end?

05 · Fluent Use: Real-Life Time Challenges

15 minutes · Practice both scenarios, approximately 6 minutes each, then reflect for 3 minutes.

Speaking timer

6 minutes per scenario · 3 minutes for reflection

06:00

Ready when you are.

Scenario 1 · Daily Life: Planning a Birthday

You and a friend are planning a birthday celebration. Agree on the time, preparations, activities, and cleanup.

Discuss: What happens before the party? What happens during it? What will you do afterward? Who will help on the day?

Mission: Agree on a clear timeline and explain it aloud.

Scenario 2 · BPO: Your First Day at a Contact Center

Partner A: You are a new teammate. Ask questions about the schedule.

Partner B: Explain this fictional training-day schedule and answer follow-up questions.

Sample schedule: 8:00 welcome · 9:00 training · 10:30 break · 11:00 practice calls · 12:30 lunch · 1:30 coaching · 3:00 review

Mission: Explain what happens at the beginning, during training, after lunch, and at the end of the day.

Success criteria

Use at least four time expressions; present events in a logical order; ask follow-up questions; add one detail or reason without reading a script.
